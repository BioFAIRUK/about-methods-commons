## A partnership of three

The consortium spans three partners, each owning the platform(s) it already
leads internationally:

- **University of Manchester** — WorkflowHub, RO-Crate, the Shared Project
  Space and the Observatory.
- **Earlham Institute** — Galaxy and cloud infrastructure.
- **Seqera** — Nextflow and community.

It is structured as a **"Team-of-Teams"** — a Technical DevOps team, a
Community/Concierge team, and a Curation & Observatory team — overseen by a
Management Board (the Project Lead, Project Co-Leads from each partner, and
the Product Owner). The Project Lead also sits on BioFAIR's central Delivery
Team for cross-spoke alignment.

## Heritage

The team draws on 20+ years of jointly building the FAIR workflow ecosystem:
the originators of Taverna, CWL, myExperiment and WorkflowHub (Manchester);
core Galaxy developers and operators of the Earlham RPO Galaxy server
(Earlham); and the founders of Nextflow and nf-core, now behind the Seqera
Platform used by 150+ organisations (Seqera). The same group co-defined the
Workflow Run RO-Crate standard and the FAIR principles for workflows, and
collectively run long-standing national and international services —
WorkflowHub, ELIXIR TeSS, FAIRDOMHub and CyVerse UK — that the Methods
Commons builds on.
