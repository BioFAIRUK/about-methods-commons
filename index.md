## What the Method Commons is for

Methods are often the least reproducible part of a study — buried in
supplementary PDFs or lost when a project ends. The Method Commons treats
methods as first-class, citable objects, making research across BioFAIR:

- **Findable** — one place to describe and discover how work was done.
- **Accessible** — openly available, with stable links.
- **Interoperable** — described with shared vocabularies and metadata.
- **Reusable** — versioned, licensed and citable.

## Part of the BioFAIR Commons

BioFAIR is built on three connected Commons. The **Method Commons** sits
alongside them:

- **Data Commons** — findable, interoperable datasets.
- **Method Commons** — the protocols and workflows that produced and
  analysed that data. *(You are here.)*
- **People Commons** — the researchers, roles and expertise behind the work.

Together they make research across the programme reproducible end to end.
